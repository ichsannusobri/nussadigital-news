## What moved markets

**Chipmakers reversed.** The KOSPI dropped 191.18 points to 6,889.74, falling below 7,000 during the session. Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix each fell about 4%, [Seoul Economic Daily reported](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/09/28/kospi-falls-270-percent-to-close-at-688974).

In Tokyo, the Nikkei briefly rose above 67,000 before profit-taking in semiconductor and AI-related shares pulled it down 486.58 points. That ended a five-session winning streak, according to [News On Japan](https://newsonjapan.com/article/150884.php). Dividend-related buying ahead of Wednesday's record date supported banks and insurers.

**Oil climbed again.** Brent rose about 2.5% to near $107 a barrel in Asian trading, [AP reported](https://www.ksat.com/business/2026/09/28/asian-benchmarks-are-mixed-despite-wall-streets-rally-finish-last-week/). Uncertainty over the conflict involving Iran and access to the Strait of Hormuz continues to drive prices. Higher oil adds to inflation worries and keeps pressure on bond yields. AP noted that the US 10-year Treasury yield recently touched 5.22%, its highest since 2007.

## Indonesia

The rupiah closed at **Rp17,978 per dollar**, down 75 points or 0.42%, according to Bloomberg data cited by [Suara](https://www.suara.com/bisnis/2026/09/28/160745/dana-asing-kabur-dari-indonesia-rupiah-melemah). Lukman, an analyst at Doo Financial Futures, told the outlet that the weakness reflected higher dollar demand from importers, foreign outflows and a stronger US dollar index (NDNews translation). Other regional currencies also fell: the Thai baht lost 0.57%, the Korean won 0.44% and the ringgit 0.26%.

On the stock market, the IHSG {{IHSG_SENTENCE}} The index had already fallen 3.09% last week and is down about 28% so far in 2026, [detikFinance reported](https://finance.detik.com/bursa-dan-valas/d-8679061/ihsg-ditutup-melemah-ke-level-6-200) on Friday.
